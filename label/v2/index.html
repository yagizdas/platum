About these comparisons

These are combinations of 1–4 dishes from saved Arcisstraße menus, with varied portions and nutrition. Judge the whole lunch: what you’d enjoy eating, its size, macros and price. There is no correct answer.

“Both bad” means you wouldn’t choose either lunch. It records two rejected plates. Choosing A or B records your preference and that you would eat the chosen plate; it does not mark the other plate as bad. Skip if you’re unsure.

Nutrition is estimated. Meat-dish weight includes sauce and accompaniments; it is not the amount of pure meat. Dishes with unclear dietary labels are reported separately. Calories from fat are calculated as fat grams × 9 ÷ calories.

Choices are stored on this device, with no account or automatic sync. Export a backup to keep a copy or restore it on another device. Your original labeler’s progress is kept separately.
