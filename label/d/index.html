How similarity works

Core dishes count 80%; accompaniments count 20%. D walks down up to 100 eligible ranked dish combinations, skipping a candidate if it is at least 65% similar to any earlier selection. It stops at five picks and keeps fewer when necessary.

Within each group, weighted dish overlap measures sameness. A dish reaches full presence at 150 g; smaller additions count proportionally. Extra scoops do not create novelty. Two empty groups have full agreement. Dish identities are exact, so chicken and köfte are different.

Cached main-course roles identify core dishes, including complete pasta dishes and vegetarian entrees. Standing rice and potato salad, sides, soup and dessert are accompaniments. These classifications are shown on each plate. Changing the core matters more than swapping a side.

Goal scoring, dietary filters and the 900 g limit excluding soup stay in place. D uses no LLM curation. Nutrition and portion weights are estimates.
