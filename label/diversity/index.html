What am I judging?

“Worth offering both” means the second plate adds a useful choice: a different eating experience, price, portion or nutrition trade-off. “Mostly redundant” means one option would usually be enough. You are judging the pair, rather than picking a winner.

If a plate does not suit the displayed goal, tick its suitability flag and save that separately. This does not label the two plates as similar or different. Skip when you are unsure; skipped pairs return after the others.

This pilot uses real saved Arcisstraße menus and estimated portions, prices and nutrition. There are no suggested answers. Your labels will support a future gradient-boosted classifier; saving them does not change the live recommendations.

Labels stay on this browser and device, with no account or automatic upload. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices. Restore merges labels from this exact frozen dataset. These labels are separate from the older preference and AI-review tools.
