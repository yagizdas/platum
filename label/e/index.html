    About this experiment

    E searches up to 100 eligible dish combinations and retains the best-ranked plate first. Each next pick maximizes λ × bounded quality − (1 − λ) × maximum similarity to earlier picks. Quality first uses λ = 0.80, Balanced 0.50, and More variety 0.35. These are experiment settings, not learned optimal values.

    Quality is 1 − (best score − candidate score) ÷ scale. Scale is the score range in this eligible pool, with a minimum of one preference-score unit. This preserves linear score gaps, avoids flattening clearly worse options, and avoids stretching tiny differences across zero to one. It is not a calibrated probability. Raw preference scores and original ranks remain visible.

    Similarity uses exact dish overlap, with core dishes weighted 80% and accompaniments 20%. A dish reaches full presence at 150 g; extra scoops do not create novelty. Groups absent from both plates contribute no bonus; weights normalize over groups present on either plate. Complete pasta dishes and vegetarian entrees can be cores; standing rice and sides remain accompaniments.

    Goal limits, dietary filters and the 900 g maximum excluding soup stay in place. E uses no LLM curation, embeddings or new classifier. Portions, prices and estimated nutrition are unchanged.
