    About G

    G uses F’s learned preference and acceptability models for Cut and Budget. It searches every valid combination of 1–4 dishes, with a maximum of 900 g excluding soup, and keeps the best accepted portioning of each dish combination. MMR then considers the top 100 per goal and dietary filter. Rejected plates never enter this pool.

    F’s best-ranked lunch remains first. Each later pick maximizes λ × bounded quality − (1 − λ) × maximum similarity to earlier picks. Quality first uses λ = 0.80, Balanced 0.50, and More variety 0.35. These settings can be compared; they are not learned optimal values.

    Quality is 1 − (best score − candidate score) ÷ scale. Scale is the score range of this pool, with a minimum of one preference-score unit. Similarity uses exact dish overlap: core dishes count 80% and accompaniments 20%, normalized over groups present on either plate. A dish reaches full presence at 150 g, so extra scoops do not create novelty.

    G can offer fewer than five lunches when fewer accepted combinations exist. It adds no calorie ceiling and uses no LLM curation. Budget’s model uses assistant-generated labels; G remains a staging candidate for review.
